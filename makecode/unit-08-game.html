8.1 Game 積木總覽

Game 分類提供完整的 2D 遊戲引擎，包含精靈（Sprite）、碰撞偵測、計分系統等功能。

積木參數說明
create spritex (0-4), y (0-4)在指定位置建立精靈
sprite movedirection, speed讓精靈持續移動
sprite change xdx (number)移動精靈的 x 位置
sprite change ydy (number)移動精靈的 y 位置
sprite set positionx, y設定精靈位置
sprite bounce if on edge—碰到邊緣時反彈
on sprite of kind overlapskind A, kind B兩種精靈重疊時觸發
change score byvalue (number)增減分數
score—取得目前分數
set life tovalue (number)設定生命数
change life byvalue (number)增減生命
on life zero—生命歸零時觸發
start countdownms (number)開始倒數計時
on countdown end—倒數結束時觸發

8.2 建立精靈

let player = create sprite at 2 4

在座標 (2,4) 建立一個精靈。精靈預設以小方塊顯示。

精靈種類（Kind）

種類說明
Player玩家控制的角色（預設）
Projectile子彈或投射物
Food食物或道具
Enemy敵人
Sprite一般精靈
let enemy = create sprite of kind Enemy at 0 0
enemy move left with speed 1

8.3 移動精靈

手動移動

let player = create sprite at 2 4

on button A pressed
  player change x by -1

on button B pressed
  player change x by 1

自動移動

let enemy = create sprite of kind Enemy at 4 2
enemy move left with speed 1
enemy bounce on edge

move 讓精靈持續移動，bounce on edge 讓精靈碰到邊緣時自動反彈。

8.4 碰撞偵測

精靈重疊事件

on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Enemy
  game.addScore 1
  otherSprite delete

當玩家精靈碰到敵人精靈時，加 1 分並刪除敵人。

重疊檢查積木

積木說明
sprite overlaps otherSprite檢查兩個精靈是否重疊
on sprite of kind A overlaps B當 A 和 B 重疊時觸發事件

8.5 計分系統

on start
  game.setScore 0

on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Food
  game.addScore 1
  otherSprite delete
  music play sound insert coin
積木說明
game.setScore 0重設分數為 0
game.addScore 1分數加 1
game.score取得目前分數

8.6 生命系統

on start
  game.setLife 3

on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Enemy
  game.changeLife by -1
  otherSprite delete
  player set position to 2 4

on life zero
  game.gameOver false

設定 3 條命，每次碰到敵人減 1 條命，生命歸零時遊戲結束。

積木說明
game.setLife 3設定初始生命数
game.changeLife by -1增減生命
game.life取得目前生命值
game.gameOver win遊戲結束（true=勝利, false=失敗）

8.7 倒數計時

on start
  game.startCountdown 30000
  game.setScore 0

on countdown end
  game.gameOver false

開始 30 秒倒數，時間到則遊戲結束。

8.8 範例：接球遊戲

let player = create sprite at 2 4
let ball = create sprite of kind Food at 2 0

on start
  ball move down with speed 1
  ball bounce on edge
  game.setLife 3

on button A pressed
  player change x by -1

on button B pressed
  player change x by 1

on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Food
  game.addScore 1
  otherSprite set position to randint 0 to 4 0
  music play sound magic wand

on sprite of kind Food overlaps otherSprite of kind Player
  game.changeLife by -1
  otherSprite set position to randint 0 to 4 0

JavaScript 對應程式碼：

let player = sprites.create(img`
  . . . . .
  . . . . .
  . . # . .
  . # # # .
  . . . . .
`, SpriteKind.Player)
player.setPosition(2, 4)

let ball = sprites.create(img`
  . . . . .
  . . . . .
  . . # . .
  . . . . .
  . . . . .
`, SpriteKind.Food)
ball.setPosition(2, 0)
ball.setVelocity(0, 30)
ball.setFlag(SpriteFlag.BounceOnWall, true)

game.setLife(3)

input.onButtonPressed(Button.A, () => {
  player.x -= 1
})
input.onButtonPressed(Button.B, () => {
  player.x += 1
})

sprites.onOverlap(SpriteKind.Player, SpriteKind.Food, (sprite, otherSprite) => {
  game.addScore(1)
  otherSprite.setPosition(randint(0, 4), 0)
  music.play(music.melodyPlayable(music.builtInPlayableMelody.DING), music.PlaybackMode.InBackground)
})
